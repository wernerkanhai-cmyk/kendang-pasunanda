## Description (English — App Store)

**Kendang Pasunanda** is a professional sequencer and solfège tool for Sundanese kendang — the traditional double-headed drum at the heart of West Javanese gamelan music.

Compose, practice, and study kendang patterns with a notation system rooted in the Pasunanda tradition. Whether you are a student, a performer, or a researcher, Kendang Pasunanda gives you a precise, tactile, and musical way to work with this repertoire.

**Key Features**

• **48-slot grid** — 1 measure = 4 beats = 48 slots, matching the rhythmic density of kendang notation. Single and double beams indicate 8th and 16th note groupings automatically.

• **Drum Pad & Keyboard Input** — Tap drum zones directly or use keyboard shortcuts (N, C, X, V, A, J, ;, :, L, G, F, S) for fast entry. Right-click or double-click any cell for a sound selection menu.

• **Practice Mode** — Activate the golden circle icon to lock the grid. Note input is blocked while playback and looping remain active — perfect for focused repetition.

• **Song & Library Management** — Save songs with names and folders. One-click overwrite. Export a single song or your entire library as a `.kendang` file.

• **Snippets** — Save, name, reuse, and manage short rhythmic fragments across your songs.

• **Vox Mode** — Switch from drum sounds to solfège syllables. Combinations like Dong + Pak are automatically recognised and sung as "Bang".

• **Loop System** — Set a bar-accurate loop on the ruler, lock individual sections, or combine multiple sections into a single continuous loop.

• **Tempo Track** — Per-section static or gradually accelerating tempo with a visual node editor.

• **PDF Export** — Export your notation as a printable PDF with adjustable layout settings.

• **Bluetooth & AirPlay sync** — Fine-tune the cursor offset so the visual cursor stays locked to the audio, even on wireless speakers.

## Description (Nederlands — App Store)

**Kendang Pasunanda** is een professionele sequencer en solfège-tool voor de Sundanese kendang — de traditionele dubbele trom in het hart van de West-Javaanse gamelanmuziek.

## Description (Bahasa Indonesia — App Store)

**Kendang Pasunanda** adalah sequencer dan alat solfège profesional untuk kendang Sunda — drum tradisional yang menjadi jantung gamelan Jawa Barat.

Buat komposisi, berlatih, dan pelajari pola kendang dengan sistem notasi yang berakar pada tradisi Pasunanda.

**Fitur Utama**

• **Grid 48-slot** — 1 birama = 4 ketukan = 48 slot. Balok tunggal dan ganda menandai pengelompokan 1/8 dan 1/16 secara otomatis.

• **Drum Pad & Input Keyboard** — Ketuk zona drum atau gunakan pintasan keyboard untuk entri cepat.

• **Mode Latihan** — Aktifkan ikon lingkaran emas untuk mengunci grid. Input nada diblokir; pemutaran dan loop tetap aktif.

• **Manajemen Lagu & Perpustakaan** — Simpan lagu dengan nama dan folder. Ekspor ke file `.kendang`.

• **Snippet** — Simpan, beri nama, gunakan kembali, dan kelola fragmen ritmis pendek.

• **Mode Vox** — Suku kata solfège alih-alih suara drum. Kombinasi seperti Dong + Pak otomatis dinyanyikan sebagai "Bang".

• **Sistem Loop** — Atur loop per birama pada penggaris atau gabungkan beberapa bagian.

• **Ekspor PDF** — Ekspor notasi sebagai PDF yang dapat dicetak.
